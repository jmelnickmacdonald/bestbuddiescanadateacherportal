Chapter idea · Meetings Elementary & Middle

Low-pressure first meeting: activity stations

Set up a game table, colouring, music, and a quieter conversation space, then let members choose where they want to begin.

What worked

Choice took pressure off conversation and made it easier to join gradually.

Next time

Use three stations instead of four so the room feels a little calmer.

Chapter idea · Leadership High School

Let student leaders own recruitment

Give the student executive the poster, announcements, lunch-table recruitment, and welcoming role instead of having the Staff Advisor run every piece.

What worked

Students talked about the chapter in their own voice and brought friends with them.

Next time

Start one week earlier and give each leader one clear recruitment job.

Chapter idea · Inclusion Elementary & Middle

Make “watch first” a real option

At the start of an activity, tell the whole group that they can watch a round, pass, or join when they are ready. It makes the option feel normal instead of exceptional.

What worked

Members could see how the activity worked before deciding how they wanted to take part.

Next time

Put the participation choices on a small visual card too.

Chapter idea · Friendship High School

A question jar for the quiet moments

Put easy conversation prompts in a jar and leave it on a table. Buddy pairs or small groups can use it when they want something to talk about, without making it a whole-group activity.

What worked

It gave people somewhere to start without putting anyone on the spot.

Next time

Add a few non-verbal choice prompts, like choosing between two pictures or activities.

Chapter idea · Celebrations Elementary & Middle

Build a year-end memory wall together

Invite members to add a drawing, sentence, photo, symbol, or favourite moment from the year. The wall grows during the last few meetings instead of becoming one big final project.

What worked

There were lots of ways to contribute, and it gave everyone something to look back on.

Next time

Start it earlier so there is more time for members to add to it.

Chapter idea · Fundraising High School

Pick one fundraiser and let students run the plan

Instead of collecting a long list of possibilities, choose one realistic fundraiser and give student leaders clear pieces of the plan to own.

What worked

Keeping the goal small made it easier to get started and easier to divide the work.

Next time

Choose the date earlier and assign communication roles at the same meeting.
