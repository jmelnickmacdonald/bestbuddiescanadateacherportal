December at a glance

What matters this month.

A few things to keep moving this month.

01

Keep the rhythm simple

A short, enjoyable chapter meeting is enough. You don't need to add another complicated event to the school calendar.

02

Give everyone a role

Make contribution visible through building, creating, choosing, guessing, moving, communicating, or helping the group in another way.

03

Notice what teamwork needs

Use the close to name what helped the group listen, cooperate, solve problems, or include one another.

✓

December does not need to become a production. One good shared activity can be enough.
