What do I do if…?

Type a word like “Abuse,” “VSC,” “teacher,” “reference,” or “registration.”

The teacher didn’t receive the registration email.

Confirm the school’s unique link in Salesforce and send it directly.

They didn’t get the Abuse Policy email.

Yearly page → check “Send Abuse Policy” → Save → ask them to check their inbox.

A new teacher is taking over the chapter.

Introduce / re-pitch the program, offer support, update the contact, and consider starting monthly.

A new Buddy is not a client of the host organization.

Request a professional reference from someone who has worked with them for at least one year.

A Peer Buddy has no registration access.

Confirm the chapter is active and the student is enrolled at the school. Then send the application link.

A student asks if they need another reference this year.

If a valid reference was submitted previously, it is a one-time requirement and does not need to be repeated.

A VSC was submitted previously.

Check timing. Peer Buddies / Chapter Leaders renew every 2 years.

A participant completed training.

Ask for the completion certificate and upload it to the Salesforce application.

A teacher has left or is on leave.

Do not close the chapter too quickly. Find the replacement advisor, re-introduce the program, offer practical support, and update Salesforce.

A school seems overwhelmed restarting.

Support retention where possible. A new advisor can start small, for example once a month, and increase frequency as they get comfortable.

North star: keep chapters active, supported, and safe while making the process easier for schools, students, Buddies, and Peer Buddies.
