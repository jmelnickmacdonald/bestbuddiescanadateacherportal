There’s more than one way to take part.

You don’t have to turn the Walk into another major project. Start with the option that fits your chapter, your school, and the time you have.

Option 1

Join a community Walk

If there’s a Walk near you, your chapter can register a school team and join the celebration. Community Walks are free to attend.

Fundraising

Keep it manageable.

If your chapter wants to fundraise through the Walk, it doesn’t need to become a second job. Sharing your team link is a simple place to start. If students want to do more, choose one idea that fits naturally into the school day.

Share the team link

Send it to students, staff, and families who may want to join the team or make a donation.

Pick one simple idea

A small school fundraiser can be enough. There’s no need to build a full calendar of events.

Ask for a hand

Your Program Advisor can help with the team page, materials, fundraising ideas, or figuring out what makes sense.
