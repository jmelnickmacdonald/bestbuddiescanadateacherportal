October at a glance

What matters this month.

A few things to keep moving this month.

01

Bring the chapter together

Plan a welcoming Meet & Greet or early chapter meeting and give people time to settle in.

02

Complete current training

Use Learn @ Best Buddies for the current Sensitivity Training rather than recreating it in the Hub.

03

Learn what members enjoy

Notice interests, strengths, communication, and the kinds of activities people actually want to do.

04

Keep the door open

Continue recruitment and family communication where your chapter still needs it.

✓

Choose what fits your group. You do not need to do everything at once.
