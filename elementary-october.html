October Getting to Know You

October is where the chapter starts spending real time together. The Meet & Greet can be simple. The goal is a welcoming first connection, not a perfect event.

October priorities

Make space to get to know each other.

Keep these pieces in front of you first. The activity bank and meeting builder below are there to help you carry them out, not to add another layer of requirements.

  1. 1
    Plan and host your Meet & Greet.

    Create a welcoming first gathering where new and returning members can meet, learn more about the chapter, and begin building connections.

  2. 2
    Complete Sensitivity Training.

    Use the current Best Buddies Canada training through Learn @ Best Buddies.

  3. 3
    Keep families informed and continue recruitment where needed.

    Share program updates with parents and guardians and keep the door open for students who are still interested in joining.

  4. 4
    Learn about interests, strengths, and availability.

    Use the first gatherings to learn what participants enjoy and, where it applies to your chapter, what may help with future buddy connections.

The October Program Calendar offers several Meet & Greet ideas, including a friendship time capsule, bingo, skills sharing, icebreakers, an All About Me activity, a chapter poster or mural, and optional seasonal activities.

Keep it welcoming

Connection matters more than cramming in activities.

One good activity plus time to arrive, settle in, and spend time together can be enough.
