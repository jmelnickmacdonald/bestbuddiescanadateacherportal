Best Buddies is a global nonprofit organization that creates opportunities for friendship, leadership, inclusion, and belonging for people with intellectual and developmental disabilities and their peers. Through our school programs, students have opportunities to build genuine friendships, take part in shared activities, develop leadership skills, and help strengthen inclusion within their school community.

What is Best Buddies?

Best Buddies brings students together to build friendships and create a more inclusive school community.

Every chapter looks a little different. Depending on the age of students and the way the school chooses to run its program, activities may include:

  • Shared lunches and group activities
  • School events and social gatherings
  • Opportunities to build meaningful one-to-one friendships
  • Student leadership opportunities
  • School-wide inclusion and awareness activities

Some chapters focus mainly on group activities, while others, particularly at the secondary level, may also include one-to-one friendships based on shared interests. The program is flexible so schools can create something that works well for their students and community.

Why participate?

Best Buddies gives students opportunities to make new friends, build confidence, strengthen social connections, develop leadership skills, and take an active role in creating a more welcoming and inclusive school community.
