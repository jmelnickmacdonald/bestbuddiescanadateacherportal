Parent & Guardian Information

Dear Parents and Guardians,

What is Best Buddies?

Best Buddies is a friendship program. It brings students with and without intellectual and developmental disabilities together. Students get to know each other, do activities, and make friends. Everyone should feel welcome and included.

What will students do?

Students may:

  • Eat lunch together.
  • Play games or join group activities.
  • Take part in school events.
  • Spend time with a friend who enjoys the same things.
  • Help plan activities.

Each school plans its own Best Buddies activities. Some schools have group activities. Others also match two students to spend time together as friends.

Why join?

Your child can meet new people, make friends, and practise new skills.
