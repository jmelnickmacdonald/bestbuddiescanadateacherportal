September Getting Set Up

September is about building the leadership team, recruiting members, and giving the chapter a clear first rhythm. You do not need to rush matching or build a big event before the group is ready.

Do not carry this alone.

Student leaders can own real pieces of recruitment and planning. The Staff Advisor keeps the adult responsibilities and supports the team.
