September

Getting
Set Up

Build the team, welcome new members, and give student leaders meaningful responsibility from the beginning.

This month

Program Calendar priorities

  • Check in with your Program Coordinator.
  • Recruit chapter members and promote Best Buddies to new students.
  • Share program information and registration information with parents and guardians.
  • Coordinate and plan your first Meet & Greet.
September focus

Start with the people.

September is a good time to recruit, reconnect, and build the student leadership team before worrying about a big event.

Recruitment

Invite Students In

Use announcements, lunch information tables, classroom outreach, and student-to-student invitations to build awareness.

Leadership

Build the Team

Identify a Chapter President and students who can take meaningful responsibility for chapter life.

Planning

Prepare the First Gathering

Get the Meet & Greet on the calendar and let student leaders help shape what it could look like.

Student leaders can own this

Give them real work worth leading.

  • Recruitment announcements and outreach
  • Welcoming interested members
  • Ideas for the first chapter gathering
  • Helping decide what chapter culture should feel like

A useful September question

Before solving the next chapter task, ask your student leaders: “What do you think we should do?”

October

Getting To
Know You

Use the Meet & Greet to help members connect, find common ground, and begin learning more about one another.

This month

Program Calendar priorities

  • Plan and host your Meet & Greet.
  • Complete Sensitivity Training.
  • Share program information and updates with parents and guardians.
  • Continue recruitment as needed.
  • Learn about students’ interests, strengths, and availability for future buddy pairings where applicable.
Ready-to-run ideas

Make the first gathering easy to join.

Give people something comfortable to do together, then leave room for natural conversation and connection.

Meet & Greet

Friendship Bingo

Help members meet people and discover shared interests.

Student-led

Skills Showcase

Invite members to share an interest, skill, hobby, or something they enjoy.

Conversation support

All About Me

Interests, favourites, and preferences can make first conversations easier.

Shared project

Chapter Mural

Create something together that represents the chapter.

Student leaders can own this

Let them help run the room.

  • Welcoming members as they arrive
  • Explaining or leading activities
  • Helping make sure new students are included
  • Gathering ideas for the next chapter meeting

Staff Advisor role

SUPPORT THE CHAPTER. LET STUDENTS LEAD.

Student leadership should strengthen the chapter and make your Staff Advisor role more manageable.

Staff Advisor Adult oversight, school and family communication, program contact, safety, and practical guidance.
Chapter President Helps lead meetings, communication, recruitment, and chapter planning.
Leadership Team Can own meaningful pieces of recruitment, activities, welcoming members, and chapter culture.
Best Buddies Provides resources, training, the Program Calendar, and ongoing program support.
Support

NEED A HAND?

Your Program Advisor can help with student leadership, matching, chapter planning, school-specific questions, and anything that needs more context than a webpage.
