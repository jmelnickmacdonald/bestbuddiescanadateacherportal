Student leadership

YOU DON’T HAVE TO CARRY THE CHAPTER.

High School chapters are built for student leadership. The Chapter Leadership Team can plan activities, gather member ideas, organize chapter meetings, and keep momentum going, while the Staff Advisor provides the adult guidance the school context requires.

Chapter Leadership Team Bring ideas forward, plan chapter activities, share responsibilities, and ask members what they want.
Staff Advisor Provide adult oversight, school communication, privacy and safety support, and guidance when students need it.
Best Buddies Provides the Program Calendar, guides, training, activity ideas, and ongoing support for the chapter.
