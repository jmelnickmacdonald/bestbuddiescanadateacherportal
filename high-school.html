High School

Support the chapter without having to carry every part of it. Student leaders can take meaningful ownership while you provide the guidance, consistency, and adult support that help the chapter thrive.

Your role

Support the chapter. Let students shape it.

Strong student leadership can make the chapter better and make the Staff Advisor role more manageable.

Staff Advisor Provides adult oversight, supports planning, communicates with Best Buddies and the school, helps with family communication, and keeps the chapter moving in a safe and realistic way.
Chapter President Helps lead the chapter, coordinate meetings, encourage participation, communicate with members, and work with the Staff Advisor and leadership team.
Leadership Team Can take meaningful responsibility for recruitment, activities, communications, chapter culture, welcoming members, and other pieces of chapter life.
Best Buddies Provides the Program Calendar, training, resources, program guidance, and support from your Program Advisor throughout the year.

A little extra context

Help students turn the calendar into chapter life.

The Program Calendar gives your chapter the year’s structure. These notes focus on where student leadership can take the lead and where Staff Advisor support matters.

You do not have to own every task. Look for real pieces of chapter life that students can lead with your support.

September

Getting set up

September is a good time to build the team, welcome new students, and decide how student leaders will help carry the chapter forward.

Program Calendar focus

Getting Set Up

  • Check in with your Program Coordinator.
  • Recruit chapter members and promote Best Buddies to new students.
  • Share program information and registration information with parents and guardians.
  • Coordinate and plan your first Meet & Greet.
Student leadership

September is a good time to hand over real responsibility.

Student leadership works best when students are trusted with meaningful parts of the chapter, not just small jobs.

Recruitment

Let students invite students.

Chapter leaders can help with announcements, lunch information tables, recruitment posts, classroom visits, and welcoming interested members.

Planning

Ask them what the chapter should feel like.

Before planning every meeting, ask student leaders what would make people want to come back and what would help new members feel welcome.

Staff Advisor

Guide without taking everything back.

Help students think through timing, accessibility, school expectations, and practical details while leaving room for them to make decisions.

October

Getting to know you

The Meet & Greet gives students a chance to connect before the year becomes too structured. A little planning can make connection easier without scripting every interaction.

Program Calendar focus

Getting to Know You

  • Plan and host your Meet & Greet.
  • Complete Sensitivity Training.
  • Share program information and updates with parents and guardians.
  • Continue recruitment as needed.
  • Learn about students’ interests, strengths, and availability for future buddy pairings where applicable.
Meet & Greet

Plan enough to make connection easier.

Give students a few ways to participate, then leave some room for conversations and friendships to develop naturally.

Icebreaker

Friendship Bingo

A low-pressure way for members to move around, meet people, and discover shared interests.

Shared project

Chapter poster or mural

Let members create something that represents the chapter. There are many ways to contribute beyond writing or drawing.

Conversation support

All About Me

Prompts about interests, favourites, routines, and preferences can make first conversations easier.

Student-led

Skills Showcase

Invite members to share an interest, skill, hobby, or something they enjoy. Students can decide how they want to participate.

Program training

Sensitivity Training

Current Best Buddies training supports respectful language, inclusion, communication, and participation as your chapter gets to know one another.
