High School

Support the chapter. Let students help shape it.

Best Buddies High School chapters are supported by a Staff Advisor and led with a Chapter President and student executive team. Chapters can include group activities, one-to-one friendships, or a mix that makes sense for the school.

Share the leadership

You do not have to run every piece of the chapter.

Strong student leaders can own real parts of the work while the Staff Advisor stays close enough to guide, support and step in when needed.

The current High School Manual gives substantial responsibility to the Chapter President and executive team. The Staff Advisor remains an important adult support, family contact and link to Best Buddies.

Staff AdvisorSupports planning, recruitment and matching; communicates with Best Buddies; acts as a contact for parents or guardians; oversees chapter funds.
Chapter PresidentLeads the chapter with the leadership team, schedules meetings and activities, supports recruitment, keeps Best Buddies updated and completes the roster.
Executive membersHelp plan activities, events, fundraising, communications and other chapter needs.

September

Getting Set Up

The High School Manual is explicit that recruitment should be strong in September. This month is mainly about building the team, getting information out and planning the first gathering rather than filling the calendar with events.

From the official Program Calendar

Elementary & High School checklist

  • Check in with your Program Coordinator / Program Advisor.
  • Recruit chapter members and promote Best Buddies to new students.
  • Share program information and registration details with parents or guardians.
  • Coordinate and plan your chapter’s first Meet & Greet.

Teacher companion

Three ways September can feel.

Use the lane that matches your chapter. Returning chapters do not need to pretend they are brand new, and new chapters do not need to race ahead.

New / rebuilding

Build the team first.

Recruit interested students, identify potential student leaders, share program information and get the chapter structure in place.

Returning chapter

Reconnect and hand over.

Bring returning leaders together, welcome new members and decide which pieces students can own right away this year.

Already have momentum

Make recruitment visible.

A casual lunch table, club fair presence or hallway information table can reconnect returning students and attract new members without becoming a formal event.

October

Getting to Know You

October moves from setup into connection. The calendar asks chapters to host the Meet & Greet, complete training, keep recruiting as needed and begin learning enough about members to support future buddy pairings where matching is part of the chapter.

From the official Program Calendar

Elementary & High School checklist

  • Plan and host your Meet & Greet.
  • Complete Sensitivity Training.
  • Share program information and updates with parents or guardians.
  • Continue recruiting students as needed.
  • Begin learning about members’ interests, strengths and availability to support future buddy pairings.

Teacher companion

Give student leaders a few good options, not a blank page.

These are starting points from Best Buddies’ own activity and Meet & Greet materials. Student leaders can choose, combine or adapt them.

Lunch-friendly / low prep

All About Me + Human Bingo

Use the All About Me sheet to get conversations started, then Human Bingo to help people find shared interests across the room.

Best Buddies sources: All About Me Meet & Greet worksheet; High School Activity Guide.

Chapter meeting

Two Truths and a Lie + Time Capsule

Start with a familiar icebreaker, then create a Time Capsule that gives members something personal to revisit together at the end of the year.

Best Buddies source: High School Activity Guide.

Full Meet & Greet

Welcome + Skills Showcase + open connection time

Give members something concrete to share, then leave room for conversation rather than programming every minute. Skills Showcase is included in the October Meet & Greet guidance.

Best Buddies source: October Meet & Greet guidance.

Different ways to communicate

Gesture Chain or AAC / Sign Language Bingo

Use an option from the Non-Verbal Activity Guide when you want the activity itself to make space for communication that is not speech-dependent.

Best Buddies source: Non-Verbal Activity Guide.
