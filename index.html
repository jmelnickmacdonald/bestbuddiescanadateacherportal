Welcome to the Best Buddies Teacher Hub

Friendship and inclusion can look different in every school.

This space brings together the information, resources, ideas, forms, and support that can help you create meaningful opportunities for friendship and inclusion in your school community.

Start where you are. Make it work for your community.

A chapter can be what you make of it.

You might begin with a few simple group activities, build toward one-to-one friendships, involve student leaders, or find another approach that works well for your students. You don’t have to do everything at once.

Which program are you supporting?

Choose your program and we’ll point you toward the information and resources that are most useful to you.

There isn’t one perfect way to run a Best Buddies chapter. Start with what is realistic for your community and build from there.
New to Best Buddies?

Start here.

Getting started doesn’t have to feel like another big thing on your list. We’ll help you find the information you need, understand what comes next, and get your chapter underway in a way that feels manageable.

Your chapter doesn’t have to look like anyone else’s.

Your Program Advisor can help you think through what will work best for your students, staff, and school community.
