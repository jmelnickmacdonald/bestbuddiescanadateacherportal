Working Together

November is a natural point to move beyond introductions and give members something shared to solve, create, or figure out together.

Phase Two · Team Building

The Program Guide places Week 5: Working Together at the start of Phase Two. This phase focuses on teamwork, problem-solving, and building on one another's strengths.

November at a glance

What matters this month.

Use this as the short list. The Chapter Playbook holds the whole year, while this page keeps the next few things close.

01

Shift into shared tasks

Choose activities where members need one another, rather than only taking turns answering questions.

02

Notice different strengths

Make room for members to contribute through planning, building, gesture, ideas, movement, creativity, or another role.

03

Keep teamwork flexible

Pick activities that suit your room and group. A guide activity can be swapped when its movement, reading, or group-size demands don't fit.

✓

Phase Two is about building cohesiveness. The strongest activity is the one that lets this group work together successfully.

Good November choices

Give the group something to do together.

A mix of communication styles, shared challenges, and small-team activities makes November feel different from the get-to-know-you stretch.
