The whole year at a glance

The chapter, step by step

The detailed month pages, Quick Guides, and official resources fill in the “how” when you need it. This page is here to keep the overall path clear.

Get your chapter ready

Connect with Best Buddies, get the school-side basics in place, and know who is helping lead the chapter.

Find your members

Let students know about the chapter, make it easy to express interest, and keep family communication moving.

Get everyone registered and trained

Work through the program requirements without trying to do everything at once.

Bring the group together

Choose a first gathering that feels welcoming, manageable, and flexible for the people in the room.

Get to know one another

Learn about interests, communication, strengths, preferences, and what makes time together work.

Build and support friendships

Keep making room for real connection, different ways to participate, and relationships to develop at their own pace.

Keep the chapter going

Use the monthly rhythm, member ideas, and simple activities to keep connection consistent without overplanning.

Celebrate and wrap up the year

Notice what the chapter built together, celebrate friendships, and close the year with care.
