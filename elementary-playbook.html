Communication There is more than one way to take part.

The Non-Verbal Activity Guide includes whole-chapter activities built around Sign Language, AAC, gesture, body language, and other non-speaking communication.

Use the guide first Some activities already include modifications.

Examples include assistive-speaking-device support for My Best Buddies Flag, buddy-pair reading/writing support for Bingo, and Raise Your Hand If as the guide-provided lower-movement alternative to I’m Seeking Common Ground.
