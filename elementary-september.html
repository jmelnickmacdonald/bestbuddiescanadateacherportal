September at a glance

Four things. That’s enough.

Still recruiting? No problem. September is for getting going.

01 Check in

Touch base with your Program Advisor and make sure you have what you need.

02 Recruit

Get the word out and give students an easy way to join.

03 Share with families

Send home the program and registration information families need.

04 Choose a first date

Pick a Meet & Greet date when your group is ready.

Make it theirs from the start

Ask: What would make our first gathering feel welcoming? Members can answer however works best for them: speaking, pointing, signing, AAC, drawing, writing, gesture, or another way.
