September Getting Set Up

Start small. September is mostly about getting organized, welcoming people in, and getting ready for the first chapter gathering. You do not need to have everything figured out at once.

Four things are enough.

If your chapter is still recruiting or getting organized, you are on track. Build a gathering only when your group is ready.

Let the chapter shape it

Ask students what would help a first gathering feel welcoming. Offer a few visible choices and let people answer by speaking, pointing, signing, using AAC, writing, drawing, gesture, or another way that works for them.
