September

Start with these four things.

September is mostly about getting organized. If your group is not ready for a gathering yet, that is okay. Start here and build from there.

1
Check in

Connect with your Best Buddies Program Advisor if you need support getting started.

2
Recruit

Invite students in the way that fits your school: announcements, classroom outreach, a table, or social posts.

3
Share with families

Make sure parents and guardians have the program and registration information they need.

4
Pick a Meet & Greet date

Start planning the first chapter gathering. It does not need to happen before your group is ready.

✓
If that is all you get done this month, you are on track.

Already have a group ready to meet? Open the optional planning ideas below.

Need a fresh idea later? The meeting planner is always here when you want it.
Ready to bring people together?

Don’t overthink the first gathering.

Start with one of these Best Buddies-based plans, or build your own in three easy picks.

25–30 min Easy start

Keep it simple.

Rock, Paper, Scissors→Defining Friendship→Pledge
40–50 min More connection

Give it a little more room.

Quick opener→Friendship→Pass the Yarn→Pledge
Flexible Creative

Make something together.

Imagine Boards→Pledge
Or build your own

Three picks. Done.

1
Start together

Pick an opener

Choose one. You do not need all three.

5 min · active

Ultimate Rock, Paper, Scissors

A quick, playful way to learn names and get some energy out.

15–20 min · names

Pass the Yarn

Learn names and something about each person while building a visible web of connection.

Flexible · 10+ people

Human Bingo

A good fit for a larger group that is ready to mingle and find things in common.

↓
2
Do something together

Pick one connection activity

Choose the one that feels most like your group.

15–20 min · pairs

Defining Friendship

Use words and drawings to explore what a good friend looks like.

Flexible · visual

Imagine Boards

Use words, pictures, or symbols to show hopes for the Best Buddies year.

Flexible · year-long

Time Capsule

Share a little about yourselves now and create something to revisit at year-end.

Finish together

Close simply

The Program Guide already gives you a ready-made close.

Best Buddies Pledge
