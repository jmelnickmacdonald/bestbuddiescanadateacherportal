September priorities

Start the year without overbuilding it.

These are the pieces to keep in front of you this month. The planning ideas farther down the page are optional support, not extra program requirements.

  1. 1
    Check in with your Best Buddies Program Coordinator or Advisor.

    Use your Best Buddies contact if you need help with start-up, recruitment, meetings, or anything chapter-specific.

  2. 2
    Recruit chapter members and promote Best Buddies to new students.

    Use the recruitment approach that fits your school, including announcements, social posts, information tables, or classroom outreach.

  3. 3
    Share program information and registration details with parents and guardians.

    Make sure families have the information they need for participation and registration.

  4. 4
    Coordinate and start planning your first Meet & Greet.

    Pick a date and begin thinking about what a welcoming first chapter gathering could look like.

If your chapter is still recruiting, that is okay. September can be mostly setup. If your group is ready to meet, use the ideas below to put together a simple first gathering.
