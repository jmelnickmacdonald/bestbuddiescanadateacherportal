Need a hand?

Some questions are easier to solve with a person. Your Program Advisor can help with chapter planning, recruitment and registration, meetings and participation, Friendship Walk and fundraising, or anything that is specific to your school.

Talk to your Program Advisor

If you already work with a Program Advisor, use the contact information you normally use. If you are not sure who your Advisor is, Best Buddies Canada can connect you with the right person.
