October Getting to Know You

October is where the chapter begins spending real time together. The Meet & Greet should create room for connection, not prove how many activities the leadership team can fit into one event.

Let student leaders run the room.

Give the Chapter Leadership Team visible jobs at the Meet & Greet while the Staff Advisor keeps privacy, family communication, safety, and adult oversight.
