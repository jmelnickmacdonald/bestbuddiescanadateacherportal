The whole year at a glance

The chapter, step by step

The detailed month pages, Quick Guides, and official resources fill in the “how” when you need it. This page keeps the overall path visible without putting every task on the Staff Advisor.

Get the chapter and leadership team ready

Confirm the Staff Advisor and student leadership structure, then get the year organized.

Recruit members

Let students know about Best Buddies and give student leaders meaningful ownership of recruitment where appropriate.

Register and complete training

Keep required program steps moving while protecting the Staff Advisor from becoming the only person doing the work.

Plan the first gathering

Use the leadership team to create a welcoming first chapter experience that is simple enough to run well.

Get members connected

Learn about interests, communication, strengths, preferences, and what helps members enjoy time together.

Support buddy friendships

Notice what is working, help when a friendship needs support, and leave room for relationships to develop naturally.

Let student leaders lead

Keep shifting appropriate planning, communication, and chapter-life responsibilities to the leadership team.

Keep the chapter active

Use a manageable meeting rhythm, member ideas, and ready-to-run activities instead of relying on big events.

Celebrate and wrap up the year

Recognize the friendships, leadership, and community the chapter built together.
