Elementary & Middle School

Your chapter can grow in a way that works for your school. School staff provide the structure and support, while students get the space to connect, participate, and build friendships together.

Your role

You know your students. We bring the Best Buddies lens.

You do not need to make friendship happen. Your role is to help create the conditions where it can.

School staff Provide adult guidance, organize chapter logistics, communicate with families and the school, and help students participate in ways that work for them.
Students Take part in activities, get to know one another, contribute ideas, and help shape the chapter community.
Best Buddies Provides program resources, the Program Calendar, training, practical ideas, and support from your Program Advisor throughout the year.

September

Getting set up

September can be mostly about reconnecting, recruiting, and getting the chapter organized. It does not need to begin with a big event.

Program Calendar focus

Getting Set Up

  • Check in with your Program Coordinator.
  • Recruit chapter members and promote Best Buddies to new students.
  • Share program information and registration information with parents and guardians.
  • Coordinate and plan your first Meet & Greet.

September can be simple.

Choose what fits where your chapter is right now. These are options, not additional program requirements.

New or rebuilding chapter

Focus on finding your people.

Promote the chapter, connect with interested students, share information with families, and get a first gathering on the calendar.

Returning chapter

Reconnect before you over-plan.

Give returning students a chance to reconnect and invite new members in before deciding what the first bigger chapter activity needs to be.

Already have momentum

Let students help shape the year.

Ask what they enjoyed last year, what they would like to repeat, and what they want friendship and inclusion to look like this year.

October

Getting to know you

The first Meet & Greet is about making it easier for students to connect, participate, and learn a little more about one another.

Program Calendar focus

Getting to Know You

  • Plan and host your Meet & Greet.
  • Complete Sensitivity Training.
  • Share program information and updates with parents and guardians.
  • Continue recruitment as needed.
  • Learn about students’ interests, strengths, and availability for future buddy pairings where applicable.
Meet & Greet ideas

Give people something easy to do together.

Activities can help take the pressure off conversation. Leave enough room for students to interact naturally too.

Easy to run

Friendship Bingo

Students move around the room finding people who match different prompts. It can be an easy way to learn names, interests, and shared experiences.

Creative

Chapter poster or mural

Create something together that represents the chapter. Students can draw, write, choose images, contribute ideas, or participate in another way that works for them.

Conversation support

All About Me

Give students prompts about favourite things, interests, activities, and preferences that can help start natural conversations.

Show what you enjoy

Skills Showcase

Invite students to share something they enjoy or are good at. Participation can look different for every student.

Program training

Sensitivity Training

Use the current Best Buddies training resources to support respectful language, inclusion, communication, and participation.
