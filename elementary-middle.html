September

Getting
Set Up

September can be simple. Focus on getting the chapter organized, welcoming students, and preparing for your first gathering.

This month’s checklist

Program Calendar priorities

  • Check in with your Program Coordinator.
  • Recruit chapter members and promote Best Buddies to new students.
  • Share program information and registration information with parents and guardians.
  • Coordinate and plan your first Meet & Greet.
New or rebuilding

Find your people.

Focus on awareness, interested students, family information, and getting a first gathering on the calendar.

Returning chapter

Reconnect first.

Give returning members time to reconnect and welcome new students before over-planning the year.

Already rolling

Let students shape it.

Ask what students enjoyed last year and what they want their chapter to feel like this year.

Helpful this month

Recruitment materials, family information, the Program Calendar, and your Program Advisor are the most useful places to start.

October

Getting To
Know You

October is about making connection easier and helping students get comfortable with one another.

This month’s checklist

Program Calendar priorities

  • Plan and host your Meet & Greet.
  • Complete Sensitivity Training.
  • Share program information and updates with parents and guardians.
  • Continue recruitment as needed.
  • Learn about interests, strengths, and availability for future buddy pairings where applicable.
Ready to run

Friendship Bingo

An easy way to help students move around, meet people, and discover shared interests.

Shared activity

Chapter Mural

Create something together that represents the chapter, with different ways for students to contribute.

Conversation support

All About Me

Use interests, favourites, and preferences to give students easy starting points for conversation.

Training this month

Sensitivity Training is part of the October program focus. Current training is available through Learn @ Best Buddies.
