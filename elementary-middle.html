Your role

YOU KNOW YOUR STUDENTS.

Best Buddies brings the friendship and inclusion lens. You bring the knowledge of your students, your school, and what participation looks like for your group.

School staff Provide adult guidance, school communication, logistics, and support for participation.
Students Connect, contribute ideas, make choices, and help shape chapter life in ways that work for them.
Best Buddies Provides the Program Calendar, guides, activities, training, and ongoing program support.
