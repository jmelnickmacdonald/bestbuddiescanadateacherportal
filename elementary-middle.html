September

Getting
Set Up

September can be simple. Focus on getting the chapter organized, welcoming students, and preparing for your first gathering.

This month

Program Calendar priorities

  • Check in with your Program Coordinator.
  • Recruit chapter members and promote Best Buddies to new students.
  • Share program information and registration information with parents and guardians.
  • Coordinate and plan your first Meet & Greet.
What this could look like

Start where your chapter is.

September does not need a large event. Choose the path that fits your school right now.

New or rebuilding

Find Your People

Promote the chapter, welcome interested students, share information with families, and get the first gathering on the calendar.

Returning chapter

Reconnect First

Give returning students a chance to reconnect and welcome new members before trying to plan the whole year.

Already have momentum

Ask Students

Ask what students enjoyed last year and what they want friendship and inclusion to look like this year.

October

Getting To
Know You

October is about making connection easier and helping students become comfortable spending time together.

This month

Program Calendar priorities

  • Plan and host your Meet & Greet.
  • Complete Sensitivity Training.
  • Share program information and updates with parents and guardians.
  • Continue recruitment as needed.
  • Learn about students’ interests, strengths, and availability for future buddy pairings where applicable.
Ready-to-run ideas

Give people something easy to do together.

Shared activities can make first conversations easier while still leaving room for natural connection.

Meet & Greet

Friendship Bingo

Help students move around, meet people, and discover shared interests.

Shared project

Chapter Mural

Create something together that represents the chapter, with different ways to contribute.

Conversation support

All About Me

Interests, favourites, and preferences can give students comfortable conversation starters.

Share an interest

Skills Showcase

Invite students to share something they enjoy or something they are proud of.
