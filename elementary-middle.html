For teachers supporting Best Buddies

Friendship and inclusion are at the heart of Best Buddies.

Best Buddies creates opportunities for people with and without intellectual and developmental disabilities to build meaningful friendships and stronger, more inclusive communities.

The goal stays the same. How your chapter brings it to life can reflect your students, your school, and the time you have.

Starting fresh?

Starting a chapter should feel doable.

For Elementary, Middle and High School chapters, the current process starts by checking whether your school already participates, completing the Program Application with principal approval, and connecting with Best Buddies.

1. Check your schoolSee whether an active chapter is already in place.
2. Complete the applicationUse the current Best Buddies Program Application with principal approval.
3. Connect with usA Program Advisor can help you work through what comes next.
Find a participating school Open the Program Application

You do not need to arrive with a finished chapter plan. Start with what is realistic, and build from there with Best Buddies support.
