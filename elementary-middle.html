Elementary & Middle School

Your chapter can grow in a way that works for your school.

At the elementary level, Best Buddies is an in-school friendship club. At the middle school level, the program continues those values while beginning to foster friendships between students with and without intellectual and developmental disabilities. School staff lead the chapter with support from Best Buddies.

A teacher-first view

You know your students.

This page adds the Best Buddies friendship lens and brings the pieces you may need into one place.

Best Buddies Canada’s current Elementary & Middle School materials describe the chapter as teacher-led, with support from Best Buddies staff. The program guides are there to help schools organize their chapter and offer simple activities for weekly or bi-weekly meetings.

Your school teamLeads the chapter, recruits participants, communicates with families and creates the space for students to connect.
Best BuddiesHelps chapters get started and provides program guidance, resources and ongoing support.
The chapterCan start small. Group activities are a valid way to run the program; one-to-one matching is not required for every school.

September

Getting Set Up

September is mainly about getting organized, reconnecting with Best Buddies and bringing students into the chapter. You do not need to manufacture a big event yet.

From the official Program Calendar

Elementary & High School checklist

  • Check in with your Program Coordinator / Program Advisor.
  • Recruit chapter members and promote Best Buddies to new students.
  • Share program information and registration details with parents or guardians.
  • Coordinate and plan your chapter’s first Meet & Greet.

Teacher companion

What could September look like?

You are not choosing an event package. These are three reasonable lanes depending on where your chapter is starting from.

Starting or rebuilding

Keep the focus on recruitment.

Introduce Best Buddies, invite interested students, get family information out and decide what a realistic first gathering could look like.

Returning chapter

Reconnect the group.

Welcome returning students, bring in new members and take care of the pieces that need refreshing for a new school year.

Already moving

Keep it informal.

If students are eager to reconnect, a simple lunch table or welcome-back drop-in can happen without turning September into a full event month.

October

Getting to Know You

October is where the chapter begins spending intentional time together. The focus is connection: helping students understand Best Buddies, get to know one another and build a welcoming chapter community.

From the official Program Calendar

Elementary & High School checklist

  • Plan and host your Meet & Greet.
  • Complete Sensitivity Training.
  • Share program information and updates with parents or guardians.
  • Continue recruiting students as needed.
  • Begin learning about members’ interests, strengths and availability to support future buddy pairings where applicable.

Teacher companion

Pick the kind of gathering that works for your group.

These ideas are drawn from the Best Buddies activity resources you provided. Keep the plan simple, combine pieces, or adapt them around your students.

Lunch-friendly / low prep

All About Me + Human Bingo

Give students an easy way to share interests, then use Human Bingo to help them find things they have in common with people they may not know yet.

Best Buddies sources: All About Me Meet & Greet worksheet; Elementary Activity Guide.

Full chapter session

Time Capsule + shared chapter banner

Use the Time Capsule activity to learn about one another, then create something together that gives the chapter a shared presence in the school.

Best Buddies sources: Elementary Activity Guide; October Meet & Greet guidance.

Seasonal / social

Accessible pumpkin decorating

Offer paint, markers, stickers, craft supplies or pre-cut designs so carving is not the only way to take part. Keep the activity collaborative and low-pressure.

Best Buddies source: October Meet & Greet guidance.

Different ways to communicate

Gesture Chain or Sign Language Bingo

Choose an activity that does not depend on spoken conversation. Best Buddies’ Non-Verbal Activity Guide includes options built around gesture, sign and AAC.

Best Buddies source: Non-Verbal Activity Guide.
